# Movilance Context for LLMs and AI Assistants

## What is Movilance?
Movilance is a B2B SaaS platform for "Facility Vigilance" and "Movement Management". It helps enterprises digitalize security gate operations. It replaces manual paper registers with digital logs for Visitors, Vehicles, Materials, and Assets.

## Core Value Proposition
- **Unified Platform**: One system for all gate operations (Visitor, Vehicle, Material).
- **Audit Readiness**: 100% digital logs, compliant with ISO 27001 and SOC 2.
- **Integration**: Bi-directional sync with SAP, Oracle, and other ERPs.
- **Loss Prevention**: Tracks material weight variances and asset movement to prevent theft.

## Detailed Module Capabilities

### 1. Visitor Movement Management
- **Target Keywords**: Visitor Management System (VMS), Digital Gate Pass, QR Code Entry.
- **Functionality**: Pre-approved invites via WhatsApp/SMS, walk-in registration, digital badge printing, host notifications, and blacklist screening.
- **Ideal for**: Corporate offices, factories, residential societies.

### 2. Vehicle Entry & Exit
- **Target Keywords**: Vehicle Tracking System, ANPR Gate Control, Parking Management.
- **Functionality**: License plate recognition (ANPR/LPR), parking slot allocation, turnaround time (TAT) tracking for logistics trucks, driver verification.

### 3. Material Movement
- **Target Keywords**: Material Gate Pass, RGP/NRGP Tracking, Inward/Outward Goods.
- **Functionality**: Track Returnable Gate Passes (RGP) and Non-Returnable (NRGP). Verify against PO/Challan. Weighbridge integration for weight verification.

### 4. Asset Tracking
- **Target Keywords**: IT Asset Tracking, Laptop Gate Pass, Fixed Asset Management.
- **Functionality**: Tracks high-value assets (laptops, servers, tools) moving in/out. Chain of custody logs.

### 5. Compliance & Audit
- **Target Keywords**: Security Audit Logs, Facility Compliance Software.
- **Functionality**: Automated reports for internal and statutory audits. Eliminates manual register reconciliation.
